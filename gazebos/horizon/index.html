    FAQs

    Horizon questions

    Can’t find what you need? Call 01491 612820.

    Minimal care, lasting beauty

    • Redwood: one coat of wood preserver a year
    • Cedar roof: brush off moss every few months
    What’s included in the price?

    The Horizon, delivery and installation included: EPDM flat roof; 8 × timber posts; 3 × full cladded back panels; Underside plywood; 6 × bracing supports (attached to ring beam & post); Ring beam; 8 × metal base plate supports for timber posts.

    Does the panel layout change the price?

    No. Layout changes don’t affect the price: choose which sides are open and which are clad, and your designer will confirm it with you.

    Flat roof or hipped roof?

    The flat roof is £15,200 inc. VAT: modern, with an EPDM membrane. The hipped roof is £20,700 inc. VAT, with cedar tiles for a traditional look.

    How long does it take?

    Typically 6–8 weeks from order, depending on the season. Delivery and installation included, in mainland England & Wales.

    Where can I see Crown craftsmanship?

    At our six show sites: find your nearest. Book a visit and a design consultant will meet you there.

    What base does the Horizon need?

    A level base. We’ll price the right Crown base for your ground on your quote, or you can prepare your own level base to our specification. An optional £460 site survey is available where the ground needs it.

    Do I need planning permission?

    Most garden buildings fall within permitted development. Height and position where it sits in your garden matters; your design consultant will check it with you.Planning

    Can I spread the cost?

    Yes: from £151 a month on our 10-Year Plan, 8.9% APR representative, with a 20% deposit. Buy Now, Pay Later is also available. Credit is subject to status.
