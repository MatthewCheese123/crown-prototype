    FAQs

    Hampton questions

    Can’t find what you need? Call 01491 612820.

    Minimal care, lasting beauty

    • Redwood: one coat of wood preserver a year
    • Cedar roof: brush off moss every few months
    • Thatch: weathers naturally, no maintenance
    • Plexiglass: soapy water and a soft cloth
    • Blinds & cushions: marine canvas cleaner
    What’s included in the price?

    The Premium Package is the complete Hampton: a redwood frame, your choice of cedar or thatched roof, redwood-clad underside, the nine bays in the layout you choose, full-length blinds, dining and sofa benches, two stools, a full set of cushions, the glass-topped dining and coffee tables, and delivery and installation by our own team.

    Does the panel layout change the price?

    No. Every layout is included. Choose any mix of balustrade and blind, full clad, or half clad and half plexiglass for the nine bays, and the price stays the same. Open it up for the view or shelter one side from the wind: it’s your choice.

    Cedar shingles or thatch?

    Both are included. Cedar shingles are Canadian Western Red Cedar with lead capping, and weather to a soft silver-grey; brush off moss every few months. Thatch weathers naturally and needs no maintenance.

    Which colours can I choose?

    Cushions, piping and blinds come in any mix of six colours: Green, Burgundy, Beige, Ivory, Navy and Taupe. We’ll post you fabric swatches so you can see the true colours at home.

    Can I use it all year round?

    Yes. Marine-grade blinds with PVC windows zip down against the weather, and infrared ceiling heaters with built-in spotlights keep the evenings warm.

    How long does it take?

    Typically 6–8 weeks from order, depending on the season. Installation is by our own team.

    Where can I see a Hampton?

    At three of our show sites: Bagshot · Longacres · Wickford · Alton · Woburn Sands · Frosts. Book a visit and a design consultant will meet you there.

    What base does the Hampton need?

    We lay three systems ourselves: Crown EcoGrid, a redwood deck or a composite deck on ground screws, each after a £460 site survey and ground-screw test. Or prepare your own level base to our specification.

    Do I need planning permission?

    Most garden buildings fall within permitted development. The Hampton stands 3.3m overall, so where it sits in your garden matters; your design consultant will check it with you.Planning

    How are the heaters powered?

    The infrared heaters are supplied and hung by our team; the electrical connection is made by your own electrician.

    Can I spread the cost?

    Yes: from £412 a month on our 10-Year Plan, 8.9% APR representative, with a 20% deposit. Buy Now, Pay Later is also available. Credit is subject to status.
