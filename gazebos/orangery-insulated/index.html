    FAQs

    Orangery Insulated questions

    Can’t find what you need? Call 01491 612820.

    Minimal care, lasting beauty

    • Redwood: one coat of wood preserver a year
    • Cedar roof: brush off moss every few months
    • Glass: soapy water and a soft cloth
    What’s included in the price?

    The complete Orangery Insulated, installed: Premium cedar tiled roof with vaulted ceiling internally; External & internal double-skin redwood cladding; Fully insulated building: 30mm PIR foil-backed insulation in the roof, walls and flooring, infilled between the solid 6" x 6" structural timber columns; Premium residential-grade composite SPC flooring included as standard; All doors and windows upgraded to high-grade residential aluminium triple-glazed units.

    Does the panel layout change the price?

    No. Layout changes don’t affect the price: choose where the fixed windows, half-clad sliding windows and full-clad walls go, and your designer will confirm it with you.

    Furnished or unfurnished?

    Unfurnished is £51,300 inc. VAT, ready for your own furniture. Furnished is £58,100 inc. VAT, with Crown dining and lounge furniture and cushions in any mix of six colours.

    Can I use it all year round?

    Yes. Fully insulated, with a vaulted ceiling and high-grade glazing, it is made for every season.

    How long does it take?

    Typically 6–8 weeks from order, depending on the season. Delivery and installation in mainland England & Wales, by our own team.

    Where can I see Crown craftsmanship?

    At our six show sites: find your nearest. Book a visit and a design consultant will meet you there.

    What base does the Orangery Insulated need?

    A level base. We’ll price the right Crown base for your ground on your quote, or you can prepare your own level base to our specification. An optional £460 site survey is available where the ground needs it.

    Do I need planning permission?

    Most garden buildings fall within permitted development. Height and position where it sits in your garden matters; your design consultant will check it with you.Planning

    How are the heaters powered?

    The infrared heaters are supplied and hung by our team; the electrical connection is made by your own electrician.
