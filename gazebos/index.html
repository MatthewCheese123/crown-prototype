Questions about gazebos

Planning guide →

Is installation included in the price?

Yes. Delivery and installation are included for all our gazebos across mainland England and Wales. A travel surcharge applies further away.

Will I need a base?

Yes. A concrete base is needed before we install; we provide the base specification for your contractor. An optional ground survey (£460 inc. VAT) is available if you would like your ground checked; it isn't needed for the Crown EcoGrid base.

What's included with my gazebo?

Redwood posts and handrails, a cedar-tiled roof with redwood underside cladding, marine-grade acrylic blinds with PVC windows, made-to-measure furniture and cushions, with delivery and installation included.

How long does installation take?

Typically 1 to 2 days for our small and medium gazebos; larger buildings take longer.

How long will a gazebo last?

Built to TRADA standards, with a 10-year structural guarantee covering timber rot, decay and structural integrity. Accessories carry a 1-year warranty.

Can I choose colours?

Yes: cushions, piping and canvas blinds in Green, Burgundy, Beige, Ivory, Taupe or Navy, mixed and matched.

Do I need planning permission?

Not normally: our gazebos are designed to fall within permitted development. More detail in our planning guide.

What timber do you use?

Northern European Redwood for the main structure and Canadian Western Red Cedar for the roof tiles.
