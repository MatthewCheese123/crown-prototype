What's included, and what's extra

Included
Delivery & installationBy our own teams: gazebos across mainland England and Wales; garden rooms within 100 miles of Oxford. A surcharge applies beyond those areas.
Included
Garden room specificationInsulated walls, roof and floor, pre-wired electrics and a 10-year guarantee on every collection. Contemporary adds triple-glazed aluminium doors and windows and smart air conditioning as standard; the Signature collection adds its fuller Signature specification.
Quoted separately
Ground test survey£280 incl. VAT, where your ground needs it.
Quoted separately
Foundations & utilitiesA concrete base or ground screws; for garden rooms, plumbing, electricity, water and waste can be quoted too.
Optional
ExtrasHeating, plumbing and bespoke fit-out, quoted individually.
