FAQs

Sandringham questions

Can’t find what you need? Call 01491 612820.

What sizes does the Sandringham come in?

From 4m × 3m to 10m × 5m, in half-metre steps, with a price for every size in the guide above. Other dimensions are available through our bespoke service.

What is the difference between Professional and Premium?

Both are complete, installed specifications. Premium adds an engineered oak floor, painted plasterboard walls and ceilings, Thermowood cladding, an external consumer unit, dimmer switching and brushed stainless fittings, and upgraded insulation in timber-frame builds. Compare them side by side.

Can I customise my Sandringham?

Yes. Each element can be customised or tailored, from doors and skylights to the roof, cladding and interior, or the whole room can be made bespoke to order. Your designer will price anything tailored with you.

Which doors can I have?

French, sliding or bi-fold doors, with the standard set chosen to suit the width. Standard: French doors 2m × 2m at 4m wide · bi-folds ×3 at 4.5–7m wide · bi-folds ×4 at 7.5–8.5m wide · bi-folds ×5 at 9–10m wide.

Can it meet Building Regulations, or become an annexe?

Yes, depending on the specification. The Sandringham is built to true building standards, and our bespoke service can adapt it into an annexe.

Will I need a base?

Yes: a concrete base or ground screws. Where your ground needs it, we carry out a site survey to recommend the right one for your garden, and quote it separately.

Do I need planning permission?

The Sandringham is designed to comply with permitted development in most cases. Sleeping or living in it needs planning permission. Planning guide

Is installation included?

Yes. Delivery and installation by our own specialist teams are included in both packages.
