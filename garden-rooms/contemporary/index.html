Specification

Crafted for year-round enjoyment

Fully insulated, triple glazed and pre-wired, with a generous standard specification and free interior upgrades.

ConstructionHand-built in England from 3″ × 2″ solid timber studs in FSC-certified Northern European Redwood, with double-skinned walls.
InsulationWalls insulated with 70mm membrane-backed Rockwool (134mm finished wall), a fully insulated timber-framed roof, and a floor insulated with 90mm Celotex, for comfort across the seasons.
Doors & windowsTriple-glazed aluminium doors and windows in anthracite grey with black handles, included as standard. French doors as standard; window and door positions are fixed.
Heating & coolingSmart air conditioning for cooling, heating and dehumidifying.
Electrics & lightingPre-wired to an internal consumer unit, with white sockets and switches, dimmable track lighting (3 × GU10) and 2 external up/down wall lights. Final mains connection by your own electrician (not included).
RoofFlat roof finished in premium EPDM rubber, laid in a single piece for a seamless, watertight finish.
InteriorWhite melamine walls and ceiling as standard, easy to clean and long-lasting. Free upgrades: maple or teak veneer acoustic panelling, or white-wash redwood cladding.
UsesDesigned for a garden office, gym, studio or entertaining space, all year round (non-habitable use). Planning permission is not normally required (permitted development).

Planning guidance

Do I need planning permission?

The Contemporary is designed to comply with permitted development in most cases. As a guide:

  • Up to 2.5m high if within 2m of a boundary
  • Further away: up to 3m, or 4m with a dual-pitched roof
  • Not in front of the house, and no more than half the garden built on
  • Sleeping or living in it needs planning permission
  • The Contemporary is 2.5m high overall

Guidance only. Check the Planning Portal or your local planning authority before you build.

FAQs

Contemporary questions

Can't find what you need? Call 01491 612820.

What size Contemporary garden rooms are available?

From 3m × 3m up to 7.5m × 4m, giving 9–30m² of floor space. Choose your size in the selector above.

Do I need planning permission?

In most cases, no. The Contemporary is designed to comply with permitted development, though there are exceptions depending on where you live and where you build.

Will I need a base?

Yes: a concrete base or ground screws. Where your ground needs it, we carry out a site survey to assess which suits your garden, and quote groundworks separately.

Is installation included?

Yes, within 100 miles of our Oxfordshire workshops. Beyond that, a travel surcharge applies.

How long does installation take?

A typical medium-sized standard garden room (5m × 3.5m) takes approximately 2–3 weeks.

Can I use it all year round?

Yes. The insulated 150–280mm build-up and smart air conditioning, for heating, cooling and dehumidifying, are included as standard.

Can I change the interior finish?

Yes. White melamine is standard, and maple or teak acoustic panelling or white wash redwood are free upgrades.
