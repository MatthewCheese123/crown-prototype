Crafted for year-round enjoyment

Traditional character with more light and headroom: a pitched-roof dormer and skylight, a 10-year structural guarantee, and installation by our own team.

InsulationRockwool insulation to walls and ceiling (75mm / 100mm) and an insulated floor.
Doors & windowsMaintenance-free doors and windows. French doors as standard.
Heating & coolingElectric wall heater included.
Electrics & lightingTrack spotlights with dimmer and 4 double sockets, pre-wired. Final mains connection by your own electrician (not included).
InteriorWalls and ceilings in whitewash-painted V-groove matchboard, included as standard.
UsesA garden office, studio, gym, games room or entertaining space, enjoyed through the seasons (non-habitable use). Within permitted development at 2.5m height in most cases.

Planning guidance

Do I need planning permission?

The Heritage is designed to comply with permitted development in most cases. As a guide:

  • Up to 2.5m high if within 2m of a boundary
  • Further away: up to 3m, or 4m with a dual-pitched roof
  • Not in front of the house, and no more than half the garden built on
  • Sleeping or living in it needs planning permission
  • Heritage heights: 2.5m flat roof; 3.0–3.5m pitched, as drawn on the live drawings (other sizes TBC)

Guidance only. Check the Planning Portal or your local planning authority before you build.

FAQs

Heritage questions

Can't find what you need? Call 01491 612820.

What sizes does the Heritage come in?

From 3m × 3m up to 6m × 4.5m, in half-metre steps. Choose your size in the design generator above.

Flat or pitched roof?

Both. The flat roof has a single-piece EPDM membrane. The optional pitched roof, in asphalt tiles or cedar shingles, adds a front gable dormer and more headroom. Prices for both are shown for every size above.

Is there a mezzanine option?

Crown's 3D planner offers a mezzanine on the pitched-roof Heritage. Which sizes it suits and its price at most sizes are to be confirmed by your design consultant.

Do I need planning permission?

In most cases, no. The Heritage is designed to comply with permitted development, though there are exceptions depending on where you live and where you build.

Will I need a base?

Yes: a concrete base or ground screws. We carry out a site survey to assess which suits your garden, and quote groundworks separately.

Is installation included?

Yes, by our in-house specialist teams, within 100 miles of our Oxfordshire workshops. Beyond that, a travel surcharge applies.
